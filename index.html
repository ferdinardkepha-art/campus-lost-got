About this project

A little help finding what matters.

Campus Lost & Found is a community noticeboard for reporting misplaced belongings and sharing items found around campus. It gives people a simple place to post details and get in touch to arrange an item's return.

How it works

Post whether something was lost or found, add a description and location, then browse or search notices. If a post may be yours, use its contact link to arrange a safe handoff. Meet in a public campus location and keep sensitive identifying details private.
